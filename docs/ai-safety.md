# AI Safety

TrustOS Learn is intended to become an AI-powered education platform, but AI capabilities are not yet implemented in this repository.

When AI features are introduced later, they should follow these baseline principles:

- Explain reasoning clearly where appropriate
- Respect student and teacher privacy
- Minimize personal data collection
- Acknowledge uncertainty when confidence is low
- Support English, Khmer, and bilingual usage
- Be reviewed for educational quality and safety

Additional safeguards will be required before any student-facing AI workflows are enabled in production.
